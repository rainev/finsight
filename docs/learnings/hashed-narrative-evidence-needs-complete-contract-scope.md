# Hashed narrative evidence needs complete contract scope

An exact non-AI parser can prove a filing term without proving that the claim
register is complete. When one transaction section reveals a separate fixed
payment, paid amount, or contingent maximum, preserve each economic object and
block the valuation if a material fixed obligation lacks usable timing. Never
let successful extraction of the originally expected terms hide an omitted
arrangement.

Recall this when narrative transaction evidence is added to a structural XBRL
pipeline: hash and locate the source bytes, cross-check tagged values, and audit
the whole related-contract surface before calling the company source-bound.

---
name: operating-stress-is-not-debt-or-pv
description: A source-backed bear reserve can be reproducible without becoming debt or a fabricated present-value claim.
metadata: { type: gotcha }
---
MPC's pension/postretirement and environmental carrying balances support a
reproducible bear-only stress, but the filing does not provide the full funded-
status and payment schedule needed for a finite PV reserve. **Why:** relabeling
the gross balances as debt or inventing timing would overstate source certainty;
dropping them would lose an approved downside scenario. **How to detect / apply:**
keep the gross balances, recoveries, paid cash and noncash OCF movements as
separate facts; bind the carrying balances only to the declared downside stress
and state that it is neither debt nor PV.

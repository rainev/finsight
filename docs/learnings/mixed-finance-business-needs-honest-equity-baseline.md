---
name: mixed-finance-business-needs-honest-equity-baseline
description: Use consolidated equity earnings when mortgage or finance operations cannot be separated; do not label that result FCFF.
metadata: { type: gotcha }
---
When a company combines ordinary operations with mortgage banking or another finance business, consolidated operating cash flow can mix customer financing, inventory funding, and owner cash. If the finance component cannot be reconciled separately, a conditional baseline may use source-traced consolidated earnings and an explicit earnings range/multiple, but it must be called a consolidated equity-earnings baseline. Do not call it operating FCFF, do not subtract consolidated debt through an EV bridge, and do not imply a finance SOTP was completed.

**Why:** Applying a generic FCFF bridge to PHM, DHI, or NVR either ignored mortgage debt or risked double counting it. The output looked precise while describing the wrong economic object.

**How to detect / apply:** Look for mortgage receivables, originations, loan-sale proceeds, captive-finance debt, or land financing. If those flows cannot be separated, reconstruct TTM consolidated net income from exact FY/current-YTD/prior-YTD facts, retain the finance context and accessions privately, use an equity-level baseline with Low reliability, and make the public calculator vary normalized earnings and its multiple rather than cash conversion. See [[captive-finance-fcfe-charges-equity-funded-growth-once]].

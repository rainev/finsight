IDXX's June 2026 filing describes a September 2025 asset acquisition including
an estimated $2.3M contingent payment at acquisition. It does not disclose an
"up to" ceiling, expiration, current carrying amount or settlement update.
The retained model's phrase "conservative upper bound" is an internal assumption,
not source evidence of a maximum.

Do not refresh that amount as a current liability or cap. Require a current
source-backed liability or settlement rule, retain the historical estimate in
history, and explicitly report the current evidence gap. The source-bound
selector rejects the real prior-dated fact rather than rewriting its date.

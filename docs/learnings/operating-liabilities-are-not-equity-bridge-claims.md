---
name: operating-liabilities-are-not-equity-bridge-claims
description: Do not use total-liabilities residuals as conservative financing claims when the residual consists of operating liabilities already embedded in FCFF.
metadata: { type: gotcha }
---
WDC's liabilities less debt equaled accounts payable, accruals, taxes, compensation, and other
operating liabilities. **Why:** subtracting that residual from enterprise value counts the same
obligations in working capital/earnings and again in the equity bridge. **How to detect / apply:**
reconcile debt, leases, securities, preferred/temporary equity, and NCI by filing line; operating
lease liabilities remain operating when lease expense stays in EBIT. Withhold if the financing
claims cannot be separately resolved.

WG20 adds the exception that prevents this rule from becoming a blanket zero.
TEL's $27M environmental accrual and CRWD's $17.103M loss-contingency reserve
were explicitly retained as nonroutine current claims in their approved bridge
economics. Deduct the ending reserve once while keeping the disclosed range,
provision, settlement expense and cash payments non-additive. Do not erase an
approved reserve merely because related flows pass through operating cash, and
do not add both the ending balance and its roll-forward movements.

WG28 LHX shows why recovery mechanism matters. Recognized environmental
recoverable assets are expected to recover through future government product/
service pricing, not a separately timed insurance cash payment. Matching scope
supports a related book difference, but does not by itself support net PV,
surplus cash or reversing reported OCF. Stock movements and missing payment
tags do not quantify cash. Preserve approved reserve/history until forward
payment/recovery/revenue overlap is source-reconciled.

WG29 authorized annual capture verified LHX's latest accounting policy:
reliably determinable future cash flows are generally discounted in estimating
the accrual, and some environmental expenditures are capitalized. A reported
liability is therefore not proven undiscounted gross, nor is every dollar proven
PV. Discounting an ending balance again or adding back all environmental costs
can double-count capital/cash burdens. Two complete annual packages now replace
the old uncaptured-source gap, but no quantified matched cash schedule was found;
source acquisition alone does not close forecast overlap. How to apply: bind
accounting terms and keep payment/timing unknown until actual cash allocation is
supported; preserve total capex and reported history.

---
name: predecessor-cash-history-needs-comparable-periods
description: Do not splice acquirer and target histories into a combined FCFF series unless periods, scopes, and transaction accounting reconcile.
metadata: { type: gotcha }
---
OMC's post-IPG filing explicitly says current operations, financial condition, and cash flows are
not comparable with prior periods because IPG enters only after the closing date. Adding legacy
OMC and IPG cash flows by ticker would not automatically create comparable combined owner cash.

**Why:** Fiscal alignment, eliminations, purchase accounting, transferred debt/cash, stock
consideration, integration costs, and discontinued activities can make a mechanical predecessor
sum economically false.

**How to detect / apply:** Require at least three aligned combined periods or an issuer-filed pro
forma cash-flow history; reconcile current shares and financing claims once. A full post-close year
can reopen the route. One half-year plus separate predecessor files does not clear the gate.

---
name: valuation-data-within-one-year
description: Trustworthy company data no more than 12 months old should receive at most a small reliability penalty.
metadata: { type: project }
---
For FinSight valuation reliability, source-linked company data that is no more than 12 months old must not be punished heavily merely because it is not from the latest quarter. It may still support `High` reliability when the business is stable and the carried-forward item has little effect on intrinsic value. Data older than 12 months receives a meaningful age penalty, and large business changes can justify a larger penalty even within 12 months.

**Why:** Many balance-sheet details are updated annually. Treating every non-current-quarter figure as unusable makes the publication gate stricter than the economic risk warrants.

**How to detect / apply:** Measure age from the valuation date to the source period end. Combine age with business-change and valuation-impact checks; never use age alone as the reliability decision.

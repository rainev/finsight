---
name: pre-separation-earnings-need-standalone-capital-burden
description: Cap a newly separated issuer at Conditional Low when comparative earnings did not bear its new standalone debt and no filed pro-forma bridge exists.
metadata: { type: gotcha }
---
A newly separated company's comparative earnings may be real and parent-attributable while still
being economically non-comparable. Before annualizing or normalizing them, determine whether the
older period bore the debt, interest, parent charges, and capital structure of the standalone
issuer.

**Why:** HONA's prior comparative H1 earnings did not bear the $16.006B opening standalone debt
created during the separation. Treating that period as an ordinary historical run rate would make
the earnings baseline look more stable and transferable than the filing supports.

**How to detect / apply:** reconcile the current and comparative periods to the separation date and
the cutoff-eligible financing event. Prefer a filed pro-forma or explicit allocation bridge. If none
exists but current parent-attributable earnings and shares support a finite range, use conservative
earnings/multiple scenarios, state the missing prior-period interest burden, and cap the result at
Conditional Low. Do not add a second enterprise-to-equity debt bridge to after-interest earnings.

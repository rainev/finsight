---
name: pro-forma-history-and-guidance-do-not-form-a-current-bridge
description: Historical pro forma statements and current adjusted guidance cannot be combined as one current valuation bridge when dates and transaction perimeters differ.
metadata: { type: gotcha }
---
HON's separation filing provides a March31 pro forma balance sheet and multi-year
continuing earnings. Its July earnings release separately provides HON-only Q2
cash and adjusted full-year guidance that includes Johnson Matthey and expected
divestitures. Each source is useful, but they do not share one current economic
scope for cash, debt, NCI and enterprise cash flow.

**Why:** Using the March bridge with July cash guidance would combine different
dates. Treating adjusted management FCF as enterprise FCFF would retain addbacks
and omit after-tax financing treatment. Using guidance that includes an acquisition
and pending divestitures without their funding/proceeds invents the bridge.

**How to detect / apply:** Label every pro forma and guidance value with its date,
GAAP/non-GAAP definition and event perimeter. Require one current post-close
cash/debt/NCI/share state plus enterprise-compatible cash flow before issuing a
successor. Contract payment ranges and intercompany settlement methods remain
separate until actual amounts and forecast overlap reconcile.

WG40 adds the inverse omission case. BIIB states that approximately six weeks of
post-close Apellis revenue and expense were immaterial and therefore omits pro
forma operations. Immaterial contribution to the short reported window does not
prove that acquired future cash is immaterial or make pre-close annual cash
history a post-close cash object. Keep observed acquired product revenue,
transaction cash, funding, inventory step-up, IPR&D and integration charges
separate; require filed pro forma or later comparable combined cash periods
before normalizing acquired FCFF.

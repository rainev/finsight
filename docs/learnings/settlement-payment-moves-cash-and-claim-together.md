MCK's captured June 2026 filing separately reports cash $5.164B, opioid accrual
$5.660B and subsequent July payment $496M. Payment-only arithmetic gives cash
$4.668B and claim $5.164B; the repeated $5.164B number is coincidence, not proof
of a post-period cash balance. Subtracting the payment only from the claim would
manufacture value, while deducting it twice would lose value.

How to apply: require same-program, source-backed payment evidence, timing and
currency, and move funded cash and accrued claim together once. Keep the
dollar-for-dollar movement assumption explicit, actual post-period closing
balances unknown and other cash/debt/claim movements unresolved. Do not equate
tagged cash payment with a fully reported liability rollforward. Restricted
escrow already removed from ordinary cash, historical maxima and securities
are separate economics. A narrow source-reported zero is not zero for all claims.

WG30 proves the paired engine identity in all three retained scenarios and
preserves historical values; it does not approve full NCI/preferred/legal cash
economics or publish a numeric successor.

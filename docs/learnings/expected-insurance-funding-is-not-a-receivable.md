---
name: expected-insurance-funding-is-not-a-receivable
description: Narrative insurance expectations cannot reduce a litigation claim unless a recognized same-scope recovery asset is source-bound.
metadata: { type: gotcha }
---
FIS says a securities settlement is expected to be substantially insurer funded,
but the filing does not provide a recognized numeric litigation receivable.
COO separately reports a current litigation reserve and a recognized loss-
contingency receivable, so only COO supports numeric netting.

**Why:** Treating expected insurance as cash or a receivable invents both amount
and collectability and can hide a genuine legal exposure.

**How to detect / apply:** Net only a current recognized asset tied to the same
matter, period and source scope. Keep narrative coverage expectations explicit
and review-required; never substitute zero or the gross settlement amount.

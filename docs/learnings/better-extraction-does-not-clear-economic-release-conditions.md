# Better extraction does not clear economic release conditions

The revised official-evidence pipeline recovered 35 reported, explicit-zero, or bounded fields
across the six Batch 02 holdouts and the real valuation consumer used every projected field. All
six still remained withheld because their release conditions required evidence of future or
post-event economics: comparable merger cash history, post-release conversion, completed claims
and dilution, separation allocations, a non-overlapping commitment waterfall, or a finite
transaction/standalone terminal state.

Durable rule: after an extraction upgrade, retry a withheld issuer by re-evaluating its named
economic release condition—not by counting newly populated fields. A pipeline improvement is
necessary evidence plumbing; it is not permission to invent merger, launch, separation,
commitment, or transaction outcomes.

Batch 15 adds the claim-set version of the same rule. An official DOJ agreement closed Labcorp's
previously missing settlement amount at $14.5M plus stated interest, but that did not bound the
issuer's whole current legal exposure: Ravgen future-test royalties/interest and multiple class,
ADA, and derivative matters still lacked amounts or ranges. Likewise, Align's 10%-of-turnover EU
administrative-fine ceiling did not cap private antitrust appeals, follow-on damages, patent
remedies, or indemnification. **How to detect/apply:** inventory every specifically described
material current claim; label each bounded/unbounded; release only when the complete material set
has a mutually compatible finite treatment. Never let one newly bounded subclaim stand in for the
issuer-wide claim gate.

---
name: equity-fcfe-includes-all-interest-bearing-borrowing
description: Capital-intensive FCFE must reconcile long-term debt and short-term commercial-paper borrowing before deciding that cash flow is positive.
metadata: { type: gotcha }
---
NEE appeared to have positive bounded FCFE when only long-term debt issuance and repayment were
used. Including the filing's net commercial-paper borrowing made bear and base common FCFE
nonpositive. **Why:** omitting one funding channel fabricates cash available to common equity.
**How to detect / apply:** reconcile every interest-bearing borrowing channel from the same
annual/interim periods, require total funding to remain within 0%-100% of reinvestment unless a
source explains otherwise, and withhold when the resulting base value is nonpositive.

# Policy projection is not consumer integration

A policy receipt can contain valid selected candidates and typed availability projections while the real valuation consumer still rejects or mishandles them. In FOD5, live consumer replay exposed three defects that projection tests did not: filing-table cutoff lineage was incomplete, the bridge merge rejected governed Companyfacts/table source kinds, and already-bounded annual carry-forward evidence was recomputed as a point.

Durable rule: do not claim evidence integration from a projector or sanitizer test. Replay the typed projection through `build_us_valuation`, require a field-by-field consumption ledger, persist build errors and public diffs, and keep unsafe-promotion and serving-write checks separate.

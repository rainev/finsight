---
name: contractual-ceilings-are-not-carrying-liabilities
description: CVR and milestone maxima are eligibility envelopes, not current claims; bind recognized carrying fair value separately and never add paid acquisition cash.
metadata: { type: gotcha }
---
Pharmaceutical filings can present a recognized fair-value liability beside a
larger contractual CVR or milestone ceiling. The ceiling describes what could be
paid if conditions occur; it is not a current carrying liability and must not be
probability-weighted when the filing does not disclose the probability.

GILD reports $337M of recognized MYR/Ouro carrying liabilities while separately
disclosing a $300M Arcellx CVR, $1.9B Tubulis maximum and $500M Ouro maximum.
INCY reports a $102M current carrying liability while its post-period Vega event
has $1.25B cash paid and a separate $750M future ceiling. BIIB's $273.5M carrying
total already contains the source-recognized Apellis fair-value addition.

**Apply:** reconcile aggregate, Level-3 and current/noncurrent/component views to
one carrying amount. Keep maxima/CVRs as diagnostics unless an approved scenario
explicitly replaces—not adds to—the carrying value. Keep cash already paid on a
separate dated cash bridge exactly once. Keep litigation and ongoing royalty
obligations separate until their carrying and forecast-cash overlap are proven.

For every universe-reset batch, report three exact counts out of ten: **Pass**, **Conditional**, and **Withheld**. A Pass is a source-bounded, economically suitable valuation without a material provisional dependency. A Conditional result is numeric but depends on a material governed assumption or fallback. Withheld means no defensible baseline can be produced without excessive invention.

**Why:** Batches 04–06 routed every issuer through the launch-first Conditional path, which made the watchlist grow and stopped the labels from distinguishing genuinely clean valuations from merely usable baselines. A safe number is not automatically Conditional; ordinary estimation belongs in a normal valuation, while the Conditional label is reserved for material unresolved dependencies.

**How to detect / apply:** Attempt and evidence the normal source-bounded route first for each issuer. Use Conditional only when a named material assumption remains load-bearing, and Withheld only when the uncertainty cannot be bounded. Every batch report and cumulative summary must show `Pass / Conditional / Withheld`, and the three counts must sum to the exact denominator. See [[baseline-decision-is-not-market-prediction]] and [[not-fully-recovered-is-broader-than-withheld]].

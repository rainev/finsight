---
name: disclosure-presence-is-not-economic-change
description: Separate SEC retrieval state from economic impact before labeling an approved valuation historical.
metadata: { type: gotcha }
---
An absent full capture wrapper and a material change to intrinsic-value inputs
are different facts. In the September 2026 latest-500 refresh, a broad form
gate treated ownership amendments, insider amendments, employee-plan
registration and thousands of prospectus filings as one acquisition failure.

**Why:** That collapsed retrieval completeness, filing classification and
economic validation into one status, making safe approved estimates look
financially unresolved while obscuring the smaller set of genuine changed
financial statements and transactions.

**How to detect / apply:** Freeze exact submissions metadata first. Clear only
versioned, enumerated non-economic forms or exact hash-reviewed no-change
events. Keep changed annual/interim filings, financing terms, transactions,
earnings releases and catch-all current reports in separate source/economic
lanes until their cash, debt, shares, claims and operating scope are proven.
Never infer neutrality merely from a form code or successful download.

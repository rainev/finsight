---
name: judgment-amount-is-not-carrying-liability
description: A court judgment or damages fact does not establish a current equity-bridge claim when accrual, interest, payment, appeal and recovery scope are unreported.
metadata: { type: gotcha }
---
BIIB reports a $124.3M Genentech judgment and a pending appeal through a
disclosure-only damages fact. The current filing does not separately report a
Genentech carrying accrual, judgment interest, cash payment, insurance recovery
or indemnification asset. The aggregate accrued-liability balance and unrelated
litigation expense cannot be allocated to this matter.

**Why:** deducting 100%, 50% or 0% of the judgment invents either carrying scope
or appeal probabilities. The mechanically convenient midpoint is not source
evidence.

**How to detect / apply:** maintain distinct fields for judgment/damages amount,
recognized accrual, interest, payment, same-matter recovery, possible-loss range
and appeal status. A disclosure-only judgment remains a private exposure and
forces review until the current economic claim is source-bound. Never net generic
insurance language or probability-weight an appeal without filed support.

description: Keep a learning watchlist for conditional numeric companies as well as companies that remain withheld.

During the universe reset, the Recovery Learning Watchlist includes only companies that failed the
initial batch pass and then either remained withheld after recovery or received a usable number
only through material hypothetical/event assumptions. Keep
them on the **Recovery Learning Watchlist** through Batch 50 so repeated patterns can become shared
pipeline improvements.

**Why:** A conditional number solves the immediate product need but does not prove the extraction,
claims, event, or economic-model gap has been resolved. Treating numeric as fully recovered would
discard the most valuable future learning cases.

**How to detect / apply:** After each batch's recovery, start from the initial failures, then add
every still-withheld issuer and every conditional/equity-at-risk issuer. Exclude initial passers and
remove any issuer that achieves fully source-bounded recovery. Keep the withheld register separate. Remove a
watchlist entry only after source-bounded recovery, independent verification, and user
confirmation. Related: [[conditional-values-need-economic-object-identity]] and
[[universe-reset-one-recovery-then-register]].

---
name: historical-claim-payments-do-not-prove-forward-reserve-runoff
description: Historical paid-claim rollforwards prove recurring OCF burden but not that a current noncurrent reserve is fully represented in a forward cash forecast.
metadata: { type: gotcha }
---
HCA annual filings reconcile provisions, net paid claims, reinsurance effects and
ending net reserves, so historical paid claims must not be deducted again from
reported operating cash flow. That history does not establish the multi-year
runoff of the current noncurrent reserve when the issuer says timing beyond one
year varies significantly and publishes only a next-twelve-month aggregate.

**Why:** Removing a bounded reserve sensitivity merely because annual payments
exist assumes the forward DCF contains the specific outstanding-stock runoff.
Deducting all historical payments again is also wrong because those cash effects
are already in reported OCF. These are separate overlap questions.

**How to detect / apply:** Reconcile gross/current/noncurrent reserves, recognized
recoveries, provisions and paid claims first. Keep recurring historical cash in
OCF. Clear a separate reserve sensitivity only when a current forward schedule—or
an equivalent source-linked reconciliation—shows how the outstanding reserve
runoff enters forecast cash. Never substitute an average resolution period for
payment timing.

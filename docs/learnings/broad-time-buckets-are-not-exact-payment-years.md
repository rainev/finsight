---
name: broad-time-buckets-are-not-exact-payment-years
description: A total or multi-year commitment bucket cannot be evenly split into annual payments without source timing.
metadata: { type: gotcha }
---
KLAC reports a $5.970B mixed commitment and says only that a majority is due
within twelve months. Its old 3/5/8-year equal-payment schedules were model
assumptions, not filing evidence. SNDK also reports two-year grouped buckets.

**Why:** An invented annual schedule makes a deterministic PV look source-backed
and can mix operating purchases, asset investment, leases and already-paid cash.

**How to detect / apply:** Calculate PV only from exact source-dated payments.
Keep totals and broad buckets review-required until timing and economic scope
reconcile; never evenly allocate them automatically.

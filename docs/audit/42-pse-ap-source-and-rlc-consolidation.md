## AP — source facts pass; valuation proxies do not yet pass

✔ The AP 17-Q directly confirms 30 June 2026 common shares **7,205,854,307**, cover debt **PHP325,625.209m**, cash **PHP62,658.309m**, equity-method investments **PHP138,809.675m**, and book NCI **PHP14,103.045m**. Debt reconciles as short-term **72,765.000** + long-term **247,965.118** + leases **4,895.091** = **325,625.209** (PHPm). It also confirms 1H2026 CFO **20,674.210m**, PPE/intangible cash additions **9,799.559m / 482.939m**, cash interest **9,978.697m**, and a **PHP29,480m** nonrecurring CGHI preferred-share redemption. Note 8 reports approximately **PHP3,900m outstanding contingent consideration**. The FY2025 source visually confirms PPE/intangible cash additions **27,425.617m / 1,075.736m** and cash interest **17,491.517m**. The [AP source](https://pdswordpressbucket.s3.ap-southeast-1.amazonaws.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Disclosure-No.-2960-2026-Quarterly-Report-SEC-Form-17-Q.pdf) is therefore sound for these reported inputs.

⚠ The staged [AP calculation](41-pse-ap-rlc-supervised-candidates.md) uses a **25% interest tax shield** because [BIR's general corporate rate](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%207-2025.pdf) is 25%, but the AP 17-Q reports tax provision/pre-tax income of **14.81% in 1H2026** and **17.07% in 1H2025**. Effective and marginal rates are different measures; neither pair proves the correct AP-specific interest deductibility. Holding other assumptions fixed, its calculated base moves from **PHP39.301/share at 35%** to **PHP42.439 at 25%** to **PHP45.577 at 15%**. These are one-way sensitivities, **not** new valuation estimates.

⚠ The **PHP138,809.675m JV/associate book balance is real**, and the cash-flow statement excludes equity-method earnings from CFO, so considering a separate investee component is coherent. But book carrying is **not an independent fair-value appraisal**; this single proxy contributes **PHP19.263/share** to the staged base. Holding everything else fixed and removing it drops the mechanical base to **PHP23.176/share**. The **PHP14,103.045m NCI book balance is also real**, but not an economic value for partly owned operations: doubling that claim would lower the mechanical base from **PHP42.439 to PHP40.482/share**. Neither sensitivity determines the proper JV or minority value. The reported **PHP3.9bn CGHI contingent liability** is supported, while other project/parent claims still need a complete economic bridge.

⚠ The average of FY2025 and June-2026 TTM cash FCFF in the workpaper is **arithmetically correct but not reviewed normalization**. 1H2026 receivables used PHP8.050bn of operating cash versus PHP0.752bn in 1H2025; maintenance/growth capex, working-capital reversal, project mix, and the 2026 lease-interest cash treatment remain judgmental. The active WACC/ROIC/terminal assumptions and 23 June 2026 [macro snapshot](../../backend/app/pse_valuation/config/macro_snapshot.json) are not a newly reviewer-approved September forecast. Thus **AP's source gate passes for the checked facts; its proxy/assumption approval gate does not**. The calculation-only PHP42.439 base must remain unserved and unapproved.

## RLC — consolidation-level owner bridge not found in reviewed public filings

✔ The original [RLC consolidated 17-A](https://robinsonsland.com/sites/default/files/2026-04/RLC_SEC%2017-A_December%2031%202025.pdf) gives group investment-property **cost less depreciation/impairment** of **PHP143,427.865m**, aggregate appraised fair value **PHP447,175.302m**, and dated ownership **RCR 60.51%, BRFLC 70%, AAI/GDI 51%**. Its Note 11 (printed pp. 269–270) breaks the portfolio into land, land improvements, buildings, and construction—not legal owners. The original [parent-only 20-IS](https://robinsonsland.com/sites/default/files/2026-04/Robinsons%20Land%20Corporation_%20SEC%20Form%2020-IS%20%28Definitive%20Information%20Statement%29%20Clean%20%28redacted%29_compressed.pdf) confirms parent investment-property carrying **PHP115,836.052m** and appraised fair value **PHP319,521.030m**. Subtracting parent from group gives only a diagnostic **PHP27,591.813m carrying / PHP127,654.272m fair value** residual; it is **not an identified subsidiary allocation**.

✔ The original [RCR 17-A](https://rlcommercialreit.com.ph/sites/default/files/2026-03/RCR%20SEC%2017A%20122025_to%20file.pdf) explicitly uses the **fair-value model** and carries its standalone investment properties at **PHP161,470.010m**. RLC's consolidated policy is a **cost model** for buildings, with fair value only disclosed. Consolidation uses uniform policies and common-control transfer eliminations. The [RLC parent cash-flow note, printed p. 86](https://robinsonsland.com/sites/default/files/2026-04/Robinsons%20Land%20Corporation_%20SEC%20Form%2020-IS%20%28Definitive%20Information%20Statement%29%20Clean%20%28redacted%29_compressed.pdf) separately records the 2025 common-control swap as **PHP30,675m increase in subsidiary investment** against **PHP7,606m investment-property and PHP187m PPE carrying decreases**. These are transfer/book amounts, not a post-elimination appraisal schedule. RCR's standalone fair value exceeds the entire group-less-parent diagnostic fair value by **PHP33,815.739m**; this is an accounting-scope mismatch, **not** an overvaluation amount. Applying 60.51% to all group uplift or using RCR's standalone book as group carrying would fabricate an attributable RNAV.

⚠ A **property-by-property, post-elimination, RLC-consolidated** schedule showing legal owner, group-basis carrying amount, appraised fair value, land/building rights, intra-group adjustments, and RLC effective ownership was **not found** in the reviewed group 17-A, parent 20-IS, RCR 17-A, or issuer IR materials. The filing explicitly says its subsidiaries' NCI did not require separate disclosure; it does not give this valuation bridge. No issuer was contacted and no private schedule was presumed. An exact RLC attributable uplift remains **null**. To proceed, the reviewer needs that schedule from RLC finance/IR or an equivalent reviewed appraisal-to-consolidation reconciliation; an upload from the user would suffice.

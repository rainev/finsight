## Retained mechanism and outcome

HSIC, ON and ZBH prove the shared core mechanism: current ordinary NCI plus a
separate acquisition contingent-consideration carrying liability, with
operating claims kept outside the equity bridge. TEL was inspected and excluded
because its current filing reports redeemable NCI but no ordinary-NCI carrying
balance.

HSIC and ON produced source-bound successor candidates. ZBH's current NCI,
acquisition and operating legal facts reconcile, but its China-distributor and
tax exposure is not finitely bounded, so it remains financial review and no
candidate was issued.

## BPI — strong source base, current return/capital judgment still unsigned

✔ [BPI's audited FY2025 17-A](https://www.bpi.com.ph/content/dam/bpi/documents/english/about-bpi/disclosures/sec-filings/sec-form-17-a-archive/2025%20BPI%20SEC%20Form%2017A.pdf) supports **PHP476,553m** parent-attributable equity, **PHP430,469m** prior equity, **PHP66,615m** parent-attributable income, and **5,283,794,223** end-2025 common shares. The model's endpoint-average ROE is `66,615 / ((476,553 + 430,469) / 2) = 14.6887%`; the issuer's **14.54%** annual ROE uses daily-average equity. That method distinction is not an error. The model's share denominator inferred from rounded **PHP90.19** book value/share is about **5,283.878m**, very close to 2025 reported shares. Its FY2025 **12.052% base cost of equity** is a policy rate, not a signed current BPI hurdle.

✔ The issuer's [15 July 2026 1H earnings 17-C](https://www.bpi.com.ph/content/dam/bpi/documents/english/about-bpi/disclosures/sec-filings/sec-form-17-c-new/2026/SEC%20Form%2017-C%20-%20BPI%202Q%20and%201H26%20Earnings%20Announcement_Redacted.pdf) reports **PHP32.8bn** 1H net income (−0.4% year on year), **13.8%** reported ROE, **PHP13.3bn** provisions (+84%), **2.42%** NPL ratio, **92.98%** NPL coverage, **14.0% indicative CET1** and **14.8% CAR**. It reports **5,296,362,773** common shares, **0.238%** more than 2025 year-end. The FY2025 endpoint-average 14.6887% and 1H2026 reported 13.8% are **different-period/different-average methods**, so their numerical gap is a review signal, not a direct ROE forecast adjustment. The 1H release's total equity is not automatically the same scope as the model's parent common equity. Credit costs and capital are now source-observable; how they affect normalized ROE, payout and terminal ROE is still unsigned.

**BPI readiness recommendation:** keep provisional, **nearer to a small supervised refresh than to a new model build**. Reviewer should reconcile 1H2026 parent common equity/earnings and current shares to the residual-income opening book; give one short normalized ROE/credit-cost and capital/payout judgment; and date/justify the cost-of-equity and terminal-ROE case. The 2025 book/share precision is minor; the forward return and capital judgment is material. Do not mark PHP98.438 approved from the 2025-only baseline.

## RCR — post-dilution payout check improves readiness, but not a forecast

✔ [RCR's 1H2026 17-Q](https://rlcommercialreit.com.ph/sites/default/files/2026-08/RCR%2017Q-2_Jun%202026.pdf) reports **19,548,803,008** shares as of **30 June** and **PHP4,947.148m** 1H distributable income. The [2Q2026 fund manager report](https://rlcommercialreit.com.ph/sites/default/files/2026-08/RL%20Fund%20Management%20Quarterly%20Report_Q2%202026.pdf) confirms **PHP0.1115** and **PHP0.1117** regular dividends for 1Q/2Q, totaling **PHP0.2232/share** in 1H. Twice that 1H rate is **PHP0.4464/share**, **4.59%** above the model's FY2025 **PHP0.4268/share** baseline, but **annualization is only a diagnostic**, not a 2026 forecast.

✔ A [July 2026 property-for-share disclosure](https://edge.pse.com.ph/downloadHtml.do?file_id=1945139) describes **1,287,562,302** new shares for six malls; [RCR's share page](https://rlcommercialreit.com.ph/investor-relations/share-information) reports **20,836,365,310** issued/outstanding as of **16 July**, **6.586%** above the June base. The [11 August dividend notice](https://edge.pse.com.ph/downloadHtml.do?file_id=1955573) declares **PHP0.1117/share** on the enlarged share count against **PHP2,552.006m** 2Q distributable income. `20,836,365,310 × 0.1117 = PHP2,327.422m`, **91.20%** of that income. This confirms that *one declared payout* fits the filed income even on new shares. However, the June income period ended **before** the July share issuance and does not represent a full period of earnings from the six malls; it does **not** prove the model's ongoing **2.5% growth** on the enlarged base or resolve the **11%** required equity return. A property-level appraisal NAV remains a separate check, not a substitute for the distribution model.

**RCR readiness recommendation:** keep provisional but **prioritize it for the first short approval workpaper**. The previously open dilution question is partly answered by the filed 2Q payout. One dated post-infusion distribution/share-coverage judgment, an 11% equity-cost/2.5% growth review, and a source-linked current candidate/reconciliation are the remaining economic steps. The active PHP5.147 per-share output and its PHP100,611.939m total equity value still use 2025 shares; the latter must not be presented as current post-issuance total equity without a new run. Do not change the current value from the payout check alone.

## Recommended simple working choices

| Judgment | Provisional working choice | Why it is not approval-ready |
|---|---|---|
| Interest tax shield | **25% assumed marginal rate** for the cash-interest addback, with a 20% one-way check | The [BIR rule](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%207-2025.pdf) gives 25% for corporations generally and 20% for qualifying registered enterprises under the enhanced-deductions regime. AP's annual report discusses tax-incentive eligibility, but does not establish what portion of the group's interest actually receives either shield. The 1H2026/1H2025 reported effective book-tax rates are 14.81%/17.07%; these are **not marginal borrowing tax rates**. Keep 25% as a conservative, explicit cash-flow hypothesis, not a measured AP tax fact. |
| Joint ventures and associates | **PHP138,809.675m reported equity-method carrying value**, separate from consolidated operating FCFF; do not also add their PHP11,528.280m TTM cash dividends to that FCFF | The [AP 1H2026 17-Q, Note 8 and cash-flow statement](https://pdswordpressbucket.s3.ap-southeast-1.amazonaws.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Disclosure-No.-2960-2026-Quarterly-Report-SEC-Form-17-Q.pdf) support the carrying amount and show equity-method earnings removed from CFO and dividends in investing. It is a source-bounded **book proxy, not fair value**. GNPD direct PHP46,389.217m, AA Thermal PHP41,780.142m, and CGHI PHP32,555.132m account for **87.0%** of the book balance; review those three first. Note 8 says AA Thermal also has indirect GNPD exposure, so any later look-through valuation must avoid counting the same interest twice. |
| Value owed to minority shareholders | Prefer a **PHP21,872.575m cash-distribution proxy** over the PHP14,103.045m book placeholder for an internal sensitivity only | [FY2025 AP 17-A](../../archive/local-data/pse/financial-statements/Aboitiz%20Power%20Corp/AP%20SEC%20FORM%2017-A%20-%202025%20Annual%20Report_%2804.15.2026%29_redacted.pdf) and the [1H2026 17-Q](https://pdswordpressbucket.s3.ap-southeast-1.amazonaws.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Disclosure-No.-2960-2026-Quarterly-Report-SEC-Form-17-Q.pdf) give TTM cash dividends paid to non-controlling owners of **PHP2,624.709m** and TTM minority earnings **PHP3,355.934m**, a 78.2% cash payout. A **12% cost-of-equity, zero-growth** capitalization gives PHP2,624.709m / 0.12 = PHP21,872.575m. The 12% and perpetuity are **review hypotheses**, not issuer facts; operating, concession and owner-level durability are untested. |

The AP source files and key cash/capital facts were independently checked and byte-hashed in [Audit 42](42-pse-ap-source-and-rlc-consolidation.md). In the 1H2026 17-Q, VECO and SPI accounted for approximately PHP1.52bn of the PHP1.924bn minority cash dividends, but the filed group accounts do not price every minority interest. The **12%** minority-equity hurdle is only a round review starting point above AP's staged **11.5%** base WACC, not a measured cost of equity. Book NCI is too weak to treat as economic value without at least this cash-flow challenge. Neither the cash-distribution proxy nor the book balance is a certified minority valuation.

These are **assumption tests, not bear/base/bull forecasts or new public prices**.

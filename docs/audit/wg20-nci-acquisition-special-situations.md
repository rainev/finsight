result, not release approval. WG20 did not activate production, schedule work,

## Retained mechanism and outcome

TEL and CRWD share a source-supported special-situation structure: a current
outside-owner claim, a separate acquisition carrying liability, an explicitly
reserved operating contingency, and paid or future cash that must not be added
to the current claim. TEL is the redeemable-NCI variant; CRWD is the ordinary-
NCI variant. Both produced source-bound successor candidates.

PH was inspected and excluded. Its valuation depends on a post-close $9.25B
transaction-cost overlay, $7.75B term-loan financing and a $1.5B residual
purchase-funding reserve, not a current acquisition carrying-liability rule.

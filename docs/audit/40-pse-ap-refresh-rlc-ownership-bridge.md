## RLC — RNAV ownership bridge remains unresolved

The [RLC FY2025 Form 17-A investment-property note, printed p. 270](https://robinsonsland.com/sites/default/files/2026-04/RLC_SEC%2017-A_December%2031%202025.pdf) reports consolidated carrying value **PHP143,427.865325m** and Level 3 fair value **PHP447,175.301821m**: gross uplift **PHP303,747.436496m**. The [active RLC RNAV code](../../backend/app/pse_valuation/phase_b_batch_03.py) starts from **PHP175,531.939468m parent equity**, takes 50% realization after 20% tax/leakage (0.4), and applies **100% ownership** to the resulting **PHP121,498.974598m** uplift. This produces its provisional **PHP297,030.914066m** common-equity base / **PHP61.8157 per share**; the uplift supplies **40.9%** of that base. [RLC's consolidated equity statement, printed p. 223](https://robinsonsland.com/sites/default/files/2026-04/RLC_SEC%2017-A_December%2031%202025.pdf) reports **PHP9,105.972449m book NCI**, already excluded from parent book equity, but not from the new unrecognized fair-value uplift.

RLC's 2025 report/group chart and [2026 Q1 report's dated subsidiary table](https://robinsonsland.com/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026%20RLC-SEC%201st%20Qtr%20Report.pdf) establish **60.51% RLC ownership of RCR at 31 December 2025** (55.67% by 31 March 2026; do not mix dates). The [RCR FY2025 audited accounts](https://rlcommercialreit.com.ph/sites/default/files/2026-03/RCR%20SEC%2017A%20122025_to%20file.pdf) report **PHP161,470.010490m investment properties already carried at fair value** in RCR's *standalone* statements. These standalone figures cannot be directly netted with RLC's consolidated carrying basis: intra-group transfer/revaluation eliminations and ownership effects need reconciliation. In particular, multiplying all PHP303.747bn by either 60.51% or 39.49%, or simply deducting the PHP9.106bn book NCI again, would produce an unsupported correction. The sale/share-swap and asset-transfer line in RLC's FY2025 equity statement further shows ownership/capital-recycling effects that require proper consolidation treatment.

**Minimum bridge for a corrected candidate:** split the *RLC-consolidated* property carrying and fair value by legal owning entity/asset; reconcile RCR standalone fair-value carrying to the amount RLC consolidates after eliminations; apply **each asset's effective 31 December 2025 ownership** to its own incremental uplift; apply the reviewed realization/tax policy and any parent claims once. A useful check is `attributable uplift = Σ[ownership_i × (fair_value_i − consolidated_carrying_i)]`, not `group uplift × one groupwide ownership percentage`. The audited public aggregates and RCR standalone accounts found in this review do **not** supply all asset/entity-level consolidated carrying amounts, so no numeric corrected RLC share value is claimed. The existing provisional bear/base/bull **PHP50.7533 / 61.8157 / 74.1423 per share** stay unchanged and are **not approval-ready**.

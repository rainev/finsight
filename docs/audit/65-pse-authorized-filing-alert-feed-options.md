# Audit 65 — Authorized filing-alert and feed options for AP, BPI, RCR, RLC

**Status: options staged, 2026-09-26. No subscription, inquiry, email, account connection, polling, PDF capture, catalog change or activation.** The goal is a legitimate way to learn when these four issuers file, without treating an accessible issuer/PSE website as permission for FinSight to scrape it. This review separates **filing notification** from rights to retrieve/store the full document or publish derived data.

### Cost and permission boundary

The PSE's linked [Enhanced CAF application form](https://documents.pse.com.ph/wp-content/uploads/sites/15/2021/02/PSE_Corporate_Announcements_Feed_Application_Form.pdf) currently lists a **US$2,000 one-time fee plus US$3,900 per quarter, exclusive of VAT** (US$17,600 before VAT in a first year with four billed quarters, **including** the setup fee). This is a published form amount, **not** a current binding quote or proof that email-only/four-issuer access is available at a lower price. PSE instructs applicants to send the completed form to its market-data address; FinSight has not applied. Before any spend, ask PSE to confirm current pricing, whether the four tickers can be scoped, delivery format, the exact license for machine parsing/retaining metadata, permitted original-file storage, derivative valuations, public source links, and termination/redistribution conditions.

**Decision recommendation:** For a repeatable whole-PSE discovery path, shortlist **CAF unformatted email** as the clear official product; use FTP only if licensed archival/replay is needed. If CAF economics are disproportionate for the four-company pilot, ask the four IR offices about an opt-in filing-notice list and allowed internal processing. Do not substitute direct automated website polling: [Audit 64](64-pse-issuer-metadata-polling-rights.md) found RLC's explicit restriction and no recorded grant for the other issuer sites or unlicensed EDGE use. An email notice is a trigger for a reviewed fact receipt, **not** automatic economic underwriting or approval.

## Why a corrected RLC number is still unavailable

The active RLC artifact starts with **PHP175,531.939468m** parent equity and adds **40% of the full PHP303,747.436496m group investment-property uplift**: group fair value PHP447,175.301821m less carrying PHP143,427.865325m. Its provisional base is **PHP61.815693/share**. The existing model applies **100% ownership to that uplift**, even though RCR and some other property-owning subsidiaries are partly owned. These FY2025 facts and the parent-only figures were source-checked against originals in [Audit 42](42-pse-ap-source-and-rlc-consolidation.md); this turn rechecked the artifact and arithmetic, not the original 343-page RLC PDF bytes.

The parent-only disclosed uplift is PHP203,684.978m. Subtracting parent from group leaves a **diagnostic** PHP100,062.458m uplift, but it does not identify which subsidiary owns each property or the carrying basis **after consolidation eliminations**. RCR's own FY2025 properties are already on a fair-value accounting basis, whereas RLC's group uses a different basis; the earlier [owner-bridge workpaper](41-pse-ap-rlc-supervised-candidates.md) found RCR standalone investment-property fair value PHP161,470.010m exceeding the group-less-parent diagnostic fair-value residual PHP127,654.272m. That mismatch is evidence that simple subtraction or a blanket **58.41% × group uplift** would be misleading—not a quantified overstatement.

The earlier **PHP53.486/share parent-only partial subtotal** remains a diagnostic, **not** a corrected RLC valuation, a floor, or a price target. The new RCR provisional **PHP5.310706/share** also cannot simply be added to RLC: RLC's group property and equity already include overlapping RCR interests. RLC's full attributable uplift and corrected bear/base/bull values therefore remain **null in this review**, while its existing active provisional PHP50.7533 / 61.8157 / 74.1423 per-share scenarios remain untouched.

## One missing bridge and gate

A reviewer needs **one RLC-consolidated property-by-owner schedule** for a single date: each asset's legal owner, group-basis carrying amount after transfers/eliminations, appraised fair value, land/building rights, effective RLC ownership, and reconciliation back to the group totals. That schedule would permit `sum(ownership × asset-level uplift)`, followed by the existing realization/leakage policy and parent claims once. Public filings reviewed so far give ownership percentages and aggregates, **not** this post-elimination owner allocation. The issuer lists a [2Q2026 RLC 17-Q](https://robinsonsland.com/sec-17-q-reports), but its PDF timed out or was inaccessible in this review, so its notes were **not** cleared as a possible source of the missing schedule. No issuer contact or private schedule is presumed.

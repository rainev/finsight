# Audit 64 — Issuer filing-index polling permissions

**Status: staged product decision, 2026-09-26. No legal clearance, source-registry change, external polling, issuer contact, file capture, or activation.** Scope is narrow: one low-rate automated check of issuer-hosted public filing-index pages for a filing title/date/PDF link. This does **not** decide permission to download/store original PDFs, use filing text commercially, publish source links or raw content, or show derived valuations.

| Pilot source | Source evidence checked | Staged decision for automated metadata polling |
|---|---|---|
| **RLC** | [RLC Terms of Use](https://robinsonsland.com/terms-of-use) state that the site is for personal/non-commercial use absent specific authorization and restrict automated queries of any sort and commercial screen-scraping. The [17-Q index](https://robinsonsland.com/sec-17-q-reports) is on that site. | **Do not enable** absent written permission or a separately reviewed licensed route. This is stronger than mere silence in the terms. |
| **RCR** | RCR publicly lists [SEC/PSE filing metadata](https://rlcommercialreit.com.ph/disclosures/sec-and-pse-filings). The checked footer links to a [privacy policy](https://rlcommercialreit.com.ph/privacy-policy), which addresses visitor/personal data, not a grant to automated clients. A bounded search found no RCR-specific automated-use grant. RLC owns RCR, but RLC's website terms are **not** assumed automatically to govern RCR's separate domain. | **Keep disabled pending source-owner/adviser review** of a narrow daily index-only check. Public visibility and a copyright footer do not establish permission. Do not call this prohibited as a legal conclusion. |
| **BPI** | [BPI's investor disclosure page](https://www.bpi.com.ph/about-bpi/disclosures) publicly lists annual and quarterly reports. Its [privacy policy](https://www.bpi.com.ph/privacy) concerns personal-data processing; the checked investor page did not grant automated metadata use. Online-banking terms were not treated as terms for the public investor page. The pilot registry has no configured BPI index URL. | **Keep disabled** until an appropriate rights basis and exact issuer index path are recorded. Absence of a found grant is uncertainty, not proof of a legal ban. |
| **AP** | A bounded review of [AboitizPower's public governance/disclosure information](https://aboitizpower.com/corporate-governance/policies-and-protocols) found no source-specific grant for automated issuer-index polling. The pilot registry has no configured AP index URL. Its privacy/stockholder policies do not answer metadata-reuse permission. | **Keep disabled** until the exact issuer index and rights basis are reviewed. No conclusion that all AP public pages forbid polling is made. |
| **PSE EDGE fallback** | The exchange's [EDGE disclaimer](https://edge.pse.com.ph/page/disclaimer.do) limits ordinary download/view/print to personal non-commercial use and requires prior consent for copying/storing/transferring content; it also bars overburdening the site. The [link policy](https://edge.pse.com.ph/page/linkPolicy.do) is not a reuse license. | **Do not substitute EDGE** as an unlicensed automated feed. Seek an applicable permission/licensed basis if needed. |

**Recommendation:** Leave the four `automated_metadata_access_allowed` flags `false`, with `rights_record=null`. The internal scheduler may continue processing **reviewed local bundles** without any site request; a new public link alone would be a review notice, not a valuation input. For an external feed, obtain a short written rights-owner/adviser decision covering the precise host/index, low-rate metadata polling cadence, allowed metadata retention, identification/user agent, duration, and any required attribution. Decide PDF byte capture, MinIO storage, reader-facing source links, and commercial derived-output use **separately**. An issuer email alert or licensed filing feed may be a simpler authorized discovery route, but neither was verified or enabled here.

This is a conservative **product configuration decision**, not legal advice or a legal determination of infringement. Website availability, robots behavior, a privacy notice, or an earlier manual browser review is not by itself an authorization for ongoing commercial automation. The reviewed pages and terms may change; recheck them before any later enablement.
